## Overview

In September 2025, the U.S. Federal Trade Commission (FTC) finalized a **$2.5 Billion** settlement with Amazon over deceptive Prime subscription and cancellation practices (the "Iliad" enrollment flow). 

Under the court-approved settlement, eligible current and former Amazon Prime subscribers receive cash distributions across multiple phases.

## How to Verify Genuine Payments (Whitelist)

Scammers frequently impersonate FTC refund distributors. Before clicking any link or accepting payment, verify these official identifiers:

* **Official PayPal / Venmo Sender**: `FTC Prime Subscription Settlement Fund`
* **Official Settlement Website**: `subscriptionmembershipsettlement.com`
* **Official Re-issue Portal**: `veritaconnect.com/AZF-CRI`
* **Official Support Email**: `admin@SubscriptionMembershipSettlement.com`
* **FTC Official Case Page**: `ftc.gov/refunds`

### ⚠️ Critical Scam Red Flags
1. **Never pay money to get a refund**: Neither the FTC nor Amazon will ever ask you to pay a processing fee, tax, or courier charge to release funds.
2. **Never provide banking passwords**: The settlement administrator will never call, text, or email asking for your full SSN, PIN, or bank login credentials.
3. **No urgent threats**: Genuine payments give you a standard 60-day acceptance window. Any message threatening "forfeiture within 24 hours" is fraudulent.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Why did I receive an unexpected $51 deposit via PayPal?
If you enrolled in Prime between June 23, 2019, and June 23, 2025, and used few benefits or attempted cancellation, Amazon records automatically qualified you for court-mandated compensation. No claim submission was required for Phase 1 or Phase 3.

### Q: What happens if I don't accept the PayPal or Venmo transfer?
You have until **March 5, 2027** for Phase 3 payments. Unclaimed digital transfers will be canceled, and uncashed checks will be voided and returned to the settlement common fund.

### Q: Will I get more money in Phase 4?
If total accepted funds are less than $1,000,000,000 after March 2027 reconciliation, recipients of earlier phases will automatically receive a pro-rata top-up of up to $149, bringing total compensation to $200.
